# DotAIOS Strategy

## Target problem

Independent professionals already use AI, but each agent starts without the context, standards, and workflows that make their work good. The useful options are buried in noise, and most buyers do not want to become AI setup experts.

## Our approach

DotAIOS makes a person's best setup portable through one local folder they own. Paid profession packs turn research, testing, and judgment into a small set of ready workflows, so the foundation stays broad while each offer stays specific.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Setup-averse independent consultants and freelancers. They hire DotAIOS to make client work more consistent across the agents they already use without assembling or maintaining the system themselves.

## Key metrics

- **First-value completion** - Share of buyers who complete one real workflow without live intervention, measured in pilot and support receipts.
- **Time to first value** - Median time from download to the first useful professional output, measured in onboarding receipts.
- **Outcome quality** - Share of certified workflow cases that pass the frozen outcome rubric, measured in release evidence.
- **Support burden** - Median human support touches per buyer during setup and first use, measured in the private support log.
- **Paid conversion** - Share of qualified Consultant Pack visitors who complete checkout, measured without collecting client content.

## Tracks

### Owned foundation

Keep the local folder, memory, skills, and agent activation dependable and easy to adopt.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Every paid workflow depends on one trusted setup the buyer controls.

### Curated profession packs

Select, test, package, and maintain only the workflows that materially improve one profession's work.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The value is the judgment and working configuration, not the number of files.

### Proof and distribution

Turn verified outcomes into a sparse main site, clear storefront pages, and an honest purchase-to-first-value path.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Buyers should understand the result before they encounter implementation detail.

## Not working on

- A broad marketplace before several profession packs are independently proven.
- SaaS dashboards, autonomous external actions, or a proprietary agent runtime.
- Finder simulations, terminal theatre, or dense technical storytelling on commerce pages.

## Marketing

**One-liner:** Give every agent your best setup.

**Key message:** One local foundation for your context and standards. Carefully selected profession packs add the workflows that make your agents better at the work you actually sell.

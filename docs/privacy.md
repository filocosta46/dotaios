# Privacy

DotAIOS is a local folder on your computer. This page says plainly what that means for your data.

## The software

- DotAIOS has no account, no hosted storage, and no telemetry that reports to us. The folder and everything in it stay on your machine.
- When you use the folder with an AI client, your chosen AI provider processes the context you send it. Their privacy terms apply to that processing. DotAIOS does not add a second copy anywhere.
- Optional connections are opt in and direct: GitHub sync talks to your own private repository, and the Google Workspace integration talks to Google from your machine with credentials you control. Turning them off stops the flow.
- Deleting the folder deletes the data. There is nothing on our side to request or erase.

## The website

- dotaios.vercel.app is a static site. We run no analytics scripts and set no cookies of our own.
- The hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for operating the service. See the Vercel privacy policy for details.

## Payments, when the pack goes on sale

- Checkout is not open yet. When it opens, payment will be handled by a payment provider acting as merchant of record. Card details go to them, never to us or into your folder.
- We would receive only what is needed to deliver and support the purchase: a receipt reference and the license entitlement.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU or a similar jurisdiction, your data protection rights apply against the providers that actually process your data: your AI provider, your optional connected services, and the payment provider once checkout exists. For anything about this project itself, open an issue at <https://github.com/filocosta46/dotaios/issues>.

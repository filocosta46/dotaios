IDEA 2

Sell the first finished job

Storefront
Confidence 95%Complexity Low

DescriptionLead with one concrete result: a client meeting becomes decisions, assigned actions, follow-up, and the next project update. Mention the other two workflows later.

Basisdirect: meeting-to-follow-up is `candidate`; the other two workflows are `intended`. external: Superlog leads with one job and one finished artifact.

RationaleThe offer becomes textable. Proof supports the promise instead of competing with a feature catalogue.

DownsideThe Pack appears narrower until the other workflows earn equivalent evidence.

IDEA 3

One truthful commerce rail

Storefront + mobile
Confidence 94%Complexity Medium

DescriptionPut €35 once, permanent ownership, prerequisites, and exactly one readiness-aware action in the first mobile viewport. Then show the real handoff: buy anywhere, install from the local DotAIOS folder later.

Basisdirect: checkout must stay link-free while unavailable; the commerce provider owns payment/recovery while the public CLI owns verification and install.

RationalePrice, fit, state, and next action stop being scattered across cards and caveats. Mobile intent no longer collides with a desktop-only install.

DownsideFront-loaded requirements reduce clicks, but improve buyer qualification and lower support risk.

IDEA 4

One work receipt

Proof
Confidence 93%Complexity Medium

DescriptionMake a realistic meeting result the Pack page’s only large visual: source excerpt, four usable outputs, and a compact evidence receipt. No Finder or folder chrome.

Basisdirect: the approved visual brief requires an inspectable tested workflow object; the current implementation already holds the evidence fields.

RationaleConsultants judge professional output, not an AI metaphor. Candidate status and limitations stay visible instead of being marketed away.

DownsideVisible limitations may reduce conversion until the workflow is approved.

IDEA 5

Stillness by default

Motion + accessibility
Confidence 91%Complexity Low

DescriptionRemove blanket GSAP scroll reveals. Keep only short feedback transitions and, if it earns its place, one meaningful continuity cue on the free homepage.

Basisdirect: every tagged surface currently receives the same reveal; the brief asks for calm editorial proof and warns against excessive trust-content motion.

RationalePrice, proof, and limitations are readable at first paint and remain identical under reduced motion.

DownsideTypography, spacing, and the proof object must carry the premium feel without choreography.

IDEA 6

Two routes, one truth seam

React architecture
Confidence 90%Complexity Medium

DescriptionSelect two page compositions by pathname without adding a router. Share locale, metadata, offer facts, CTA state, proof, analytics, and direct-load behavior.

Basisdirect: the app has one fixed composition, no pathname handling, root-only locale metadata, no router dependency, and no Vercel rewrite.

RationaleThis is a deep seam: one small interface hides route normalization, locale URLs, and page metadata while each page remains locally understandable.

DownsideA third or fourth content route would justify revisiting the hand-rolled selector.

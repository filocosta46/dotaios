# SEO content plan

## What is evidence today

These points come from the locked product direction and can guide copy now:

- DotAIOS is a local, user-owned folder for AI context.
- It organizes context, projects, memory, sources and skills for AI clients.
- It is for individuals such as independent consultants, freelancers, students and knowledge workers.
- It does not provide the model or replace the AI client a person already uses.
- The free core comes first. The initial paid offer is a one-time EUR 35 Independent Consultant Pack.
- AI providers may process content that a user sends through their client. The site must say this clearly.

These statements still need release evidence before they appear as capability claims:

- Exact supported operating systems, AI clients and versions.
- Install, update, removal, rollback and recovery behavior.
- The final contents and outcomes of the paid pack.
- Any claim about time saved, performance, adoption, popularity or customer results.

## What is still a hypothesis

Treat these as research prompts, not facts:

- People may search for "AI memory folder", "AI context management" or "organize files for AI".
- Independent consultants may respond better to outcome pages about client preparation and delivery than to a category page about memory.
- "Works with the AI you already pay for" may be clearer than technical language about agent memory.
- Local control and portability may matter more than a long feature list.

Validate demand, phrasing and difficulty with Search Console, a keyword source and real customer conversations. Do not manufacture search volumes or competitor comparisons.

## Position and search intent

Primary promise: Give the AI you already use a clear, private place to find your working context.

Category phrase to test: AI context folder.

Avoid: company brain, enterprise memory platform, autonomous employee, universal integration, perfect memory and "every session".

## Pillar and cluster plan

### Pillar 1: Organize context for AI

Target intent: understand the problem and set up a simple system.

Cluster drafts:

- What is an AI context folder?
- How to organize project files before using an AI assistant.
- AI memory versus source files: what should stay separate?
- How to move AI context between clients without rebuilding everything.
- A privacy checklist for sending work documents to an AI provider.

### Pillar 2: AI workflows for independent consultants

Target intent: complete a specific client task.

Cluster drafts:

- A simple AI workspace for each consulting client.
- Prepare for a client meeting with files you already have.
- Turn approved meeting notes into a follow-up draft.
- Keep proposal context separate from client delivery.
- Archive a finished client project without losing useful context.

### Pillar 3: DotAIOS guides

Target intent: evaluate, install and use the product.

Cluster drafts:

- Supported clients and tested versions.
- Install, update and remove DotAIOS safely.
- What stays local and what your AI provider may receive.
- Free DotAIOS versus the Independent Consultant Pack.
- Export, backup and recovery guide.

Publish the support and lifecycle pages before the paid checkout. Publish educational articles only when they include a real example and can link to a tested guide.

## Structured data recommendations

- Use `Organization` for DotAIOS with only verified identity and profile links.
- Use `SoftwareApplication` on the free product page once operating systems, price and download URL are accurate.
- Use `Product` with one `Offer` on the paid pack page only after checkout opens. Use EUR 35 and real availability.
- Use `Article` for guides with visible author and modified dates.
- Use `BreadcrumbList` where visible breadcrumbs exist.
- Use `FAQPage` only for questions and answers shown on the page. Do not add hidden SEO questions.
- Do not add ratings, reviews, user counts or `AggregateRating` without real source data.

Validate JSON-LD with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema.org validation before release.

## Technical and editorial checks

- One canonical URL per page and self-referencing canonicals.
- English and Italian pages have correct `hreflang` pairs, including `x-default` where appropriate.
- Sitemap, robots rules, Open Graph image and social metadata use the production domain.
- Product pages load cleanly on mobile and remain readable without JavaScript where practical.
- Screenshots have descriptive alt text. Decorative images use empty alt text.
- Each page names its audience, the job it helps with and the current limitation.
- No page implies that local files never reach an AI provider.
- No research claim appears without a source and retrieval date.

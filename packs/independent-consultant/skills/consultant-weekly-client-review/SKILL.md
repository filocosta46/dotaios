# Weekly client review

## Purpose

Turn one client's reviewed project record into a short weekly status draft and a human-owned next-step list.

## Inputs

- The exact client project and review period.
- Human-selected call notes, decisions, deliverables, and prior action lists for that client.
- The agreed scope and human-confirmed deadlines.

## Steps

1. Confirm the client and exact review dates. Read only the named project and selected files.
2. Group evidence into completed, in progress, blocked, changed, at risk, and awaiting client input.
3. Link each statement to a provenance label and source. Keep assumptions in a separate section.
4. Compare work only with the human-confirmed scope and dates. Do not infer performance ratings, commitments, or commercial consequences.
5. Draft next-step options with proposed owners, but mark every new owner and date for human confirmation.
6. Show the review, evidence gaps, and proposed project updates. Apply no update without explicit human approval.

## Output

A local weekly client review draft with status, evidence, risks, open decisions, and a review checklist.

## Safety boundary

- Work inside one named client project only. If the client scope is missing or ambiguous, stop and ask.
- Label every material input and claim as `[Client-provided]`, `[Internal note]`, `[Public source]`, or `[Assumption]`. Include a local path or citation and access date when available.
- Do not send, publish, upload, or message any output. Produce a local draft only.
- Do not recommend, set, change, or approve pricing. Leave pricing and commercial terms to the human.
- Do not read, request, store, or expose credentials or secrets. Stop and redact if one appears.
- Do not create, configure, schedule, or run automation, hooks, jobs, or background loops.
- Do not delete or overwrite source material or an existing deliverable. Propose a new draft path.
- Require explicit human review before any durable write or external action. A draft is never approval.

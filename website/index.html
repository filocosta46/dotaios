What you can ask

Same context, same skills, in every connected tool after setup.

  • Plan my day. Builds a plan from your priorities and recent work.
  • Save this article for me. Stores a page or PDF in your vault as clean text.
  • What did I decide about this project? Searches your saved notes and sessions.
  • What am I working on? Quick check that your folder is connected.

Skills install into supported tools when you run setup. Edit once in ~/aios/skills/, every connected tool sees the update.

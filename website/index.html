How it works · no command line required

It's just a folder. Your AI sets it up.

You don't run anything yourself. Your AI creates the files, asks who you are, and saves your answers where every tool can read them.

Step 1 — Tell an AI to set it up

Paste one line into the AI you already use.

It creates the folder, asks who you are and what you're working on, and writes your answers down.

What it does

More than a profile. A working memory.

Your context is the start. DotAIOS also pulls in what you read, remembers what you discussed, and follows you across devices.

Cross-device sync

Mirror your folder to a private GitHub repo you own, so an AI on your phone reads the same memory. Opt-in. No server we run.

npx dotaios sync setup

Reads the web

Hand it a URL, a PDF, or a document. DotAIOS pulls clean Markdown into your vault — JavaScript-heavy pages included.

npx dotaios ingest <url>

Remembers your sessions

Save AI conversations locally so your context compounds across tools and time. Search every past session from one place.

npx dotaios capture enable claude-code

What you can ask

Plain language. Same in every connected tool after setup.

  • Plan my day. Builds a plan from your priorities and recent work.
  • Save this article for me. Stores a page or PDF in your vault as clean text.
  • What did I decide about the launch? Searches everything you saved.
  • What am I working on? Quick check that your folder is connected.

Skills install into Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Antigravity, and Hermes when you run setup. Edit once in ~/aios/skills/, every tool sees the update.
